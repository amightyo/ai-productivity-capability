# Analysis plan

## Stage 1 — Reproduction
Reproduce the published assisted-performance and AI-removal treatment effects using the authors' primary non-Honors specification.

## Stage 2 — Matched-task panel
Construct student × assisted-task × matched unaided-task observations using the published Part 2 → Part 3 mapping. Audit conversation linkage and multiplicity.

## Stage 3 — Cognitive-engagement measurement
Develop a human-readable codebook from stratified conversation samples before automated classification. Candidate behaviors include delegation, independent attempt, hint/scaffolding, explanation seeking, verification, revision, and iterative reasoning.

## Stage 4 — Sequence analysis
Represent ordered interaction states and characterize recurring human-AI engagement pathways.

## Stage 5 — Capability models
Relate interaction pathways to matched AI-free task performance, accounting for baseline capability, treatment assignment, task characteristics, and AI reliability. Treat interaction style as post-treatment behavior and avoid unsupported causal interpretation.

## Stage 6 — Robustness and falsification
Test alternative coding schemes, linkage rules, multi-conversation handling, AI reliability measures, and model specifications. Explicitly test results inconsistent with the proposed mechanism.
